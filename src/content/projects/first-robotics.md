Team member in the FIRST Robotics Competition (1999-2003), designing and building competitive
robots under strict time and resource constraints - an early hands-on introduction to
engineering under a hard deadline that stuck with me. I spent most of my time on the team's
animation sub-team, helping render short cinematic animations of our robot succeeding at its
task in that year's competition, tied thematically to our team's identity.
