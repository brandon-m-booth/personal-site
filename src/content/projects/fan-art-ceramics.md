Games captured a part of my heart as a child and they still hold a special place there. In 2012,
I made these pieces at a DIY paint-your-own-ceramics shop. Normally I wouldn't count this as a
"project," but I spent several weekends in the shop painting all of the detail, and it turned into
a hobby for a while.
