TILES (Tracking Individual Performance with Sensors) was a large-scale, longitudinal study
tracking hospital workers' physiology, environment, sleep, and behavior using wearable and
ambient sensors, in order to understand connections between working conditions, stress, and
well-being. The project produced two publicly released datasets, TILES-2018 and TILES-2019
(see [Publications](/publications)).
